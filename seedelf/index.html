Seedelf Wallet

Private money on Cardano.

A Cardano wallet for Chrome with two sides: a public account for everyday Cardano, and a private balance where no UTxO says who owns the money. One recovery phrase opens both.

Get Seedelf for Chrome Read the source code

Inside the wallet

  • Pay any Seedelf by name

    Paste the name and the wallet finds it. The payment can't be linked to the Seedelf it pays.

  • Get paid privately

    Create a Seedelf and share its name. Anyone can pay it, and no payment points back to it.

  • It says what links

    Make money public to any address or $handle. The wallet warns you before a payment ties your accounts together.

  • A full Cardano wallet

    Send, stake with a pool and delegate your vote. Restore a Lace or Eternl phrase, or make a new one.

How it stays private

Each payment to a Seedelf reaches its owner under a fresh copy of their key, so no UTxO says who owns it, and payments to the same Seedelf can't be linked to each other.

Spending is proven with a zero-knowledge proof, so it doesn't reveal the spender either.

Amounts, tokens and timing stay public, as with any Cardano wallet, and making money private or public again links your public account to what you move. Read what Seedelf hides and what it doesn't.

dApps, privately

Use a dApp without showing it your public account. A private session funds a one-time account from your private balance, the dApp sees only that account, and what comes back goes into your private balance.

  • Swap through Minswap from a new one-time account each time
  • Connect any site to a private session, or to your public account as with any wallet
  • What comes back can go through Lovejoin, a mixer on Cardano, so it's harder to tie to the session
  • Sites can't see the wallet until you turn on connecting them in Settings

How it's built

  • Your recovery phrase never leaves your device, and it's encrypted with your password
  • Every transaction is built and signed inside the extension, in Rust compiled to WebAssembly
  • Read any transaction before you sign it, decoded on your device with nothing looked up
  • No sign-up, no analytics, no tracking
  • Open source under the MIT license
  • In English, Spanish and Japanese

Seedelf Wallet is early software, and neither Seedelf nor Lovejoin has had a third-party audit. A bug bounty pays in ADA for any flaw that can lose funds.

On the command line

seedelf-cli is a Seedelf wallet for the terminal, on Linux, macOS and Windows. It keeps its own key, apart from Seedelf Wallet's recovery phrase.
