Seedelf Wallet

A Cardano wallet for Chrome with private payments built in. The public side works like any Cardano wallet: send, receive, stake, and vote. The private side lets you pay and get paid without revealing who receives or who spends.

  • One recovery phrase for your public account and your private balance
  • Pay anyone's Seedelf by name, privately
  • Use dApps through one-time accounts, then bring the funds back to your private balance
  • Also available as a command-line wallet for Linux, macOS, and Windows
